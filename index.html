Only in Barak Valley: Hailakandi, Cachar and Sribhumi (Karimganj) districts. Outside Barak Valley, please order the PDF.

How do I pay?

PDF: tap “Get PDF”, enter your name and WhatsApp number, pay ₹100 by the UPI QR (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm or any UPI app), then send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp. Hard copy: tap “Buy hard copy”, fill in your address, pay the total (book + delivery) by the UPI QR shown, then send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp. Pay only to UPI ID 8402849739@ibl.

How fast will I get the PDF?

We send the PDF on WhatsApp after we check your payment screenshot, usually the same day.
